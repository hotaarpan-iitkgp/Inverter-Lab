Topology Working Principle

Power semiconductor switches (MOSFETs/IGBTs) are commutated according to the selected modulation strategy to synthesize alternating output voltages from the DC bus. Anti-parallel diodes ensure non-destructive paths for continuous reactive current discharge.

Analytical Formulation
• Load differential: v(t) = R · i(t) + L · (di/dt)
• Active Power: P = V_rms · I_rms · cos(φ)
• Displacement PF: cos(φ) = R / √(R² + (ωL)²)
• THD_v: √(V_rms² - V_1,rms²) / V_1,rms
Commutation Dynamics & Gate Highlighting

When gate drive signal is positive, the entire switch channel glows emerald green. If load current flows opposite to the device polarity, the anti-parallel freewheeling diode safely conducts reactive current back to the bus link.
